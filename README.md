# Future Vanity Fair Club (FVFC)

Official public organization profile for **Future Vanity Fair Club (FVFC)**.

Future Vanity Fair Club is a cyber-luxury living IP economy where 10,000 unique Vani NFTs come to life in Future City, built with 881 hand-drawn attributes and 2,000+ algorithmic generation rules.

---

## 💎 About The Project

FVFC's long-term roadmap envisions an open Living IP economy where 10,000 Vani begin as ERC-721 visual identities and gradually evolve toward programmable personas, AI agents, IP licensing, and on-chain contribution proofs.

- **Collection Size**: 10,000 Unique F.NFTs
- **Artistic Foundation**: 881 Hand-drawn vector traits across multi-layer categories
- **Aesthetic DNA**: Future Glam (Cyber-Luxury Visual Language)
- **Smart Contract**: ERC-721 Drop with on-chain revenue distribution
- **Permanent Storage**: Filecoin Network & IPFS content addressing

---

## 🏛️ Infrastructure Roadmap

The website presents FVFC through five conceptual layers. The live mint covers Vani's Visual Layer as static ERC-721 artwork; programmable persona, agent, IP, economy, and governance layers are roadmap components that evolve with the ecosystem.

```mermaid
graph TD
    FVFC[Future Vanity Fair Club]
    FVFC --- A[Visual Layer: ERC-721]
    FVFC --- B[Agent Layer: AI Agents]
    FVFC --- C[IP Layer: Story Protocol]
    FVFC --- D[Economy Layer: ERC-8004, x402, ERC-8183]
    FVFC --- E[Governance Layer: ERC-20]
```

- **Visual Layer**: ERC-721. Vani's foundational art layer, built from 881 hand-drawn traits through algorithmic generation and human refinement.
- **Agent Layer**: AI agents. Autonomous personas and programmable talents empowering Vani to create, collaborate, and evolve.
- **IP Layer**: Story Protocol. Programmable creative rights, IP licensing, derivatives, and automated royalty flows.
- **Economy Layer**: ERC-8004, x402, and ERC-8183. Standards for identity, permissions, payments, coordination, creator contributions, and IP-based rewards.
- **Governance Layer**: ERC-20. The future token layer for value exchange, contributor incentives, and decentralized governance participation.
